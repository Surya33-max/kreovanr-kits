## Website-listed products and status

The website lists ten product cards. Only publish deliverable files for products that exist as actual packages. WhipRush is marked **In Development** on the public site, Launchpad is marked **Private Beta**, and Subtitle Doctor, Form / UI Kit, Impact Zoom, Brandboard, and other concept cards must not be represented as finished downloadable products unless their source packages are created and tested. Website preview artwork is illustrative, not proof of a finished product.

## Safety and provenance

- Keep private editable client/source assets out of this public repository unless explicitly cleared for publication.
- The sanitized portfolio preview bundle is intended to omit restricted/private materials.
- Preserve original license and attribution files for third-party projects. Do not publish a modified third-party package until its license permits redistribution.
- WhipRush is based on the user's own existing work and has not been validated in a live Resolve installation in this environment.
